About these numbers

Everything here comes from the recorded sessions and laps. A lap is valid when it has no rewind and no contact and is not excluded; a time taken from the last packet counts only when the run covered the whole route. The game does not send its own verdict, its game mode or official times, so these are SplitTime’s readings of the telemetry. Top speed and other numbers that need every frame are not here yet.
