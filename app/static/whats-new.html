What’s new

Changes you can see on this site, newest first

26 October 2026

  • One report on Rivalry. Rivalry now shows just the race report for the two laps you pick. The review of a whole session (consistency, bests, where time went across its attempts, with optional AI priorities) has moved to Analysis, as “Session review” under the lap comparison: it follows the laps in the comparison tray.
  • Share a race report. While you look at two laps on Rivalry, the address in your browser names them, so you can copy it, bookmark it or send it. The strip above the report has a Copy link button too (also on Traces & corners). Opening the link goes straight to that report.
  • Menu tidied. Replay is in the menu, and so is this page. The old Rivalry page has left the menu; it is still linked at the bottom of Rivalry.
  • One pair, three views. Once you pick an A and a B lap, the same strip sits on top of the race report, Traces & corners and Replay: who, which route, both times, and one click to each of the other views for the same two laps. You can swap A and B, or go back to Rivalry. The links on Rivalry use the same three names.
  • The engineer’s review comes first. In the race report, the written review now sits right under the headline, in its own box, above the section-by-section breakdown. The notes about signing in no longer read like an error.

25 October 2026

  • Race report with an AI race engineer. For any A and B lap: where the time went, section by section, the corners that mattered, and a written review with each driver’s strengths, costs and one thing to practise. Every number in the review is checked against the measurements; a review that states anything else is not shown. Each review is written once and saved, so opening it again costs nothing.
  • Written for you on a lead change. When a route record changes hands, the review of the new record against the time it beat is written automatically, at most 20 a month.
  • Latest on Rivalry. The newest lead change, who leads how many routes, the closest gap and the latest improvements, each one click from its race report.
  • Straight from a row. Each contested route on Rivalry links to its two best laps: race report, traces and replay.
  • Sprints fixed. Corner priorities in the session debrief and the traces view now work for sprints whose recording ended at the finish line.

13–16 October 2026

  • Session debrief: a measured review of A’s whole session, with optional AI-picked priorities.
  • Replay: recorded laps raced against each other on the map.
  • Stats: twelve sections over everything recorded.
  • Rivalry: the new Rivalry page, best-lap rules, attempt counts (every started event, finished or given up), and Analysis hides empty sessions by default.
  • How it works: what the recorder does, its rules and its known faults.
